## What is RPL?
RPL is a collegiate rocketry club from the college Cal Poly Pomona. The goal of RPL is to design a lander that in simple terms can take off and land with throttle vector control.

## My Role
I am an avionics engineer on the team and have contributed to multiple static fire tests of the system and the base lander design which is currently ongoing.

### My Achievements
I have helped design 3 boards and data collection systems used during static fire tests. The boards log data from multiple sensors (highest number was 11) and communicates data to the ground control station which relays information back to control the throttle valve of the lander and actuation of solenoids for ignition and shutoff. Most recently the board I designed was used in a successful static fire test at the Friends of Amateur Rocketry base in the Mojave Desert.
<br>
I have additionally helped put together presentations for PDRs and CDRs as well as documenting the design process of the avionics system.
